## B · The mechanism one (post 2–3 days later)

> A thing we keep explaining, so here it is once.
>
> Most AI services want a card and a monthly plan. If your card doesn't work internationally, you're out — no matter how much money you have.
>
> There's a protocol that fixes this. It's called x402: an API answers `402 Payment Required`, your wallet pays for that single call, you get the response. No signup, no key, no plan. Pay for what you use, in cents.
>
> We're plugging it into SingIt so your wallet works on it directly. Same limits you already set. Same approval in your messenger.
>
> First ones landing: private AI chat, web search, data collection.

*Why it works: explains x402 without jargon and lands on "your card doesn't work" — the actual pain your users have.*
