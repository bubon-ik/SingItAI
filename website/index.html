The artifact behind every approval

{
  "agent":          "hermes-demo",
  "merchant":       "demo-merchant.local",
  "maxPerPayment":  "0.05 ALGO",
  "budget":         "1.00 ALGO",
  "expiresAt":      "2026-07-12T18:00Z",
  "nonce":          "berlin-demo-001"

Canonicalized, hashed, and signed. The agent never sees a private key.

How it works

  1. Delegate

    Tell your agent what it may spend, right in the chat: budget, merchant, expiry.

  2. Approve

    SingIt sends the policy to you on Telegram, WhatsApp, or iMessage. Strict mode adds a Firefly hardware confirmation.

  3. Pay

    The agent settles x402 payments on Algorand on its own, as long as they fit the policy.

  4. Prove

    Every payment carries the signed consent, so the merchant verifies both the money and the permission.
