How it works

Three steps, then it just buys.

  1. 01

    Sign in

    With email, Google or Apple, and we make a wallet for you. Or use the one you have: Rabby, MetaMask, Phantom, Solflare. Signing in moves nothing.

  2. 02

    Set your limits

    Pick a daily limit and a per-purchase limit. On Base we deploy a small contract that enforces them, and you pay no gas for it.

  3. 03

    Allow once

    One approval from your wallet. From then on your agent buys inside the limits. Revoke any time, in SingIt or on revoke.cash.

What it buys

Real things, paid in USDC.

Gift cards, eSIMs & top-ups

Thousands of brands through Bitrefill: games, streaming, shopping, travel data, phone credit. You see the product, price and network before it is paid, and the code is shown to you once.

Live answers

Weather, exchange rates, flight status and prices, places to eat or stay, any web page. Each answer is one small paid request to a checked seller at a capped price, counted against your limits.

A chat that bills by the answer

SingIt Ask charges what an answer actually costs, up to 0.003 USDC. Want it private? Switch to Venice, paid from the same limits.

Emails and phone calls

An email to yourself for $0.02, or an AI assistant that calls a number for you for $0.54 on Base. It says it is an AI, is not recorded and lasts at most three minutes.

Safety

A budget, not your wallet.

Your money stays with you

USDC stays in your own wallet. The agent takes only what a purchase needs, when it needs it.

Limits the agent can't argue with

On Base, a contract enforces your limits. They hold even if our server or the agent is compromised.

One signature takes it back

Revoke in SingIt, or on revoke.cash without us. An emergency stop pauses the limiter for good.

Nothing reaches people on its own

Emails and calls wait for your press. The page shows the recipient, the text and the price first.

On Solana, the chain caps the total you approve, and SingIt enforces the daily and per-purchase limits.
